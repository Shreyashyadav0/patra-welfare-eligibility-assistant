# National Scholarship Portal: merit-cum-means scholarship

The National Scholarship Portal administers centrally funded scholarships for students.

## Eligibility

The applicant must be a student enrolled in a recognised institution.

## Eligibility

Annual family income must not exceed Rs 2,50,000.

## Eligibility

The applicant must have secured at least 50 percent marks in the previous examination.

## Eligibility

The applicant must be 30 years of age or younger.

## How to apply

Apply through the National Scholarship Portal before the closing date.

## Documents required

Aadhaar, income certificate, marksheet, and bank details.
