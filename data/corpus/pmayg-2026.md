# PMAY-Gramin: assistance for rural housing

PMAY-Gramin provides assistance to rural households for constructing a pucca house.

## Eligibility

The beneficiary must reside in a rural area.

## Eligibility

Annual household income must not exceed Rs 3,00,000.

## Eligibility

The household must not already own a pucca house.

## How to apply

Registration is through the Gram Panchayat and the AwaasSoft portal.

## Documents required

Aadhaar, job card, bank account, and a certificate from the Gram Panchayat.
