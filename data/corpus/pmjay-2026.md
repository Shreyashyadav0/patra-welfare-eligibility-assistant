# Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY: health cover for low-income households

PM-JAY provides hospitalisation cover to low-income households identified for the scheme.

## Eligibility

Eligibility is limited to households with annual income not exceeding Rs 1,20,000.

## Eligibility

Households covered by ESIC or CGHS are excluded.

## Eligibility

The applicant must be at least 18 years of age.

## How to apply

Eligible families receive an Ayushman card at the nearest empanelled hospital or CSC.

## Documents required

Aadhaar, ration card, and income proof are required.
