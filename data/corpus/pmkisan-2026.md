# PM-KISAN: income support to landholding farmer families

PM-KISAN provides income support to landholding farmer families in three equal instalments each year.

## Eligibility

The applicant must be a landholding farmer family.

## Eligibility

The applicant must be at least 18 years of age.

## Eligibility

Households that pay income tax are not eligible.

## Eligibility

Serving or retired government employees are excluded.

## How to apply

Applications are made through the PM-KISAN portal or the nearest Common Service Centre.

## Documents required

Aadhaar, land records, and bank account details are required.
