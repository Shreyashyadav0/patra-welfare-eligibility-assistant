# Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana: LPG connection for eligible households

PMUY provides a deposit-free LPG connection to eligible women from priority households.

## Eligibility

The connection is issued in the name of an adult woman of the household.

## Eligibility

The applicant must be at least 18 years of age.

## Eligibility

The household must hold a BPL or equivalent category card.

## How to apply

Apply at the nearest LPG distributor with the KYC form.

## Documents required

Aadhaar, bank account, and a BPL or category certificate.
