# PM SVANidhi: working capital loan for street vendors

PM SVANidhi provides a working capital loan to street vendors in urban local bodies.

## Eligibility

The applicant must be a street vendor in an urban local body.

## Eligibility

Annual income must not exceed Rs 2,00,000.

## How to apply

Apply through the SVANidhi portal or the urban local body's vendor desk.

## Documents required

Aadhaar, certificate of vending, and bank account.
